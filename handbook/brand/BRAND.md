# BachVibe — brand seed

This is the seed the marketing agent grows into a brand book. It records what
is decided, what is inherited, and what is open. Nothing here is final until the
founder has approved it (a `needs:human` card per decision).

## What is decided

- **Name:** BachVibe. Domain `bachvi.be`. Trips live at `<slug>.bachvi.be`,
  the payer dashboard at `app.bachvi.be`, the marketing site at the apex.
- **What it is:** a private, installable app for one trip. Not a social
  network, not a planning marketplace. The pitch is *the weekend, on everyone's
  phone, in one link*.
- **Two audiences on the marketing site:** planners (one trip, buy now) and
  resellers (photographers, planners, venues — a separate page, a separate CTA).
- **Voice:** warm, quick, a little irreverent, never crude. Speaks to the
  person organising, who is stressed and doing this for a friend.

## What is inherited, and what is not

The first customer's app (Daren's Last Ride) has a complete visual system in
`Daren-bach/DESIGN.md`: 1973 tour-poster Los Angeles, paper on tolex, brass and
sunset and Penny Lane pink. **That is a theme, not the brand.** The product
ships several themes (`rock`, a bachelorette sibling, a family-reunion theme —
`BACHVIBE_PLAN.md` §T0.1 #13), and the brand has to sit *above* all of them.

What carries over: the confidence, the maximalist warmth, the idea that the
app is a physical object (a laminate, a ticket). What does not: any specific
palette or typeface from one theme.

## Open — the marketing agent's first cards

1. Logo and wordmark directions (three), each with a one-line rationale.
2. Brand palette: neutral enough to frame every theme's screenshots, warm
   enough to be BachVibe. Contrast-checked (WCAG AA), same rule as the product.
3. Type: one display face, one text face, both with a free licence.
4. Tone of voice guide: ten example lines, five things we never say.
5. The marketing site's information architecture: home, how it works, pricing,
   for resellers, themes gallery, FAQ, legal.
6. App-store-style screenshot frames per theme.

Each becomes a `type:content` card, `dept:marketing`, and lands as a section of
this file by PR once the founder approves.
